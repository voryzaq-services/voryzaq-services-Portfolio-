This page explains how Voryzaq Services uses cookies and similar technologies (like browser local storage) on voryzaq-services.github.io.

Short version: this site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. It uses one small piece of local storage to remember that you've closed the cookie notice, and it loads fonts from Google, which is a standard third-party request rather than a cookie.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser to remember information between visits. "Local storage" is a similar browser feature, often used for the same purpose, which is what this site actually uses (see below).

What We Currently Use

  • Cookie-notice preference (local storage, strictly necessary): when you dismiss the cookie banner, we store a small flag in your browser's local storage so it doesn't show again on your next visit. This isn't used for tracking and doesn't leave your browser.

What We Don't Use (Yet)

We don't currently run analytics (e.g. Google Analytics), advertising pixels, or any third-party tracking scripts on this site. If that changes in the future — for example, adding analytics to understand site traffic — this policy and the cookie banner will be updated first, and consent will be requested where required.

Embedded Project Previews

Our Portfolio page shows live previews of separate project websites (e.g. Wear Drip, Luxora, Aldermere) inside embedded frames. Those are independent websites and may set their own cookies or local storage under their own policies when loaded — this is outside our control. We'd recommend checking each project's own site for details if you interact with one directly.

Your Choices

Since this site doesn't use tracking cookies, there's nothing to opt out of beyond the cookie notice itself. You can clear your browser's local storage at any time through your browser settings, which will bring the cookie notice back on your next visit. Most browsers also let you block all cookies and site data globally if you prefer.

Changes to This Policy

We'll update this page if what we use changes — most likely if analytics are added later. Check the "Last updated" date above for the latest revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@voryzaq.com.

Note: this is a general template, not legal advice. Cookie/consent requirements vary by law and by audience (for example, the EU's ePrivacy rules are stricter than most other regions) — if you expect meaningful traffic from the EU/UK or add tracking tools later, it's worth a quick legal check to confirm this notice is sufficient.
