Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Voryzaq Services ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It applies to voryzaq-services.github.io and this portfolio's pages — not to the individual client project sites linked or embedded from our portfolio, which are each governed by their own policies (see the Third-Party Services section below).

Plain-language summary: we don't run ads, we don't sell data, and we don't currently use analytics or tracking cookies. The contact form on this site is a front-end demo and isn't yet wired to a backend — see the note in that section below.

Who We Are

Voryzaq Services is a web development and design studio. You can reach us at hello@voryzaq.com or through the contact details in this site's footer.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. Depending on how you use this site, the following may apply:

  • Contact form details — name, email address, project type and message, if you submit our contact form. Submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards them to our inbox by email. Formspree's own privacy policy governs their handling of that data in transit.
  • Direct email or social contact — anything you send us via email, Instagram or GitHub.
  • Basic hosting logs — our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages requested) as part of normal web server operation. We don't have access to detailed analytics from this by default.

How We Use It

Any information you provide is used only to respond to your enquiry, discuss or deliver a project, and maintain our business records. We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to third parties.

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. We do use your browser's local storage for one strictly functional purpose: remembering that you've dismissed the cookie notice, so it doesn't reappear on every visit. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

Third-Party Services

This site relies on a small number of third-party services to function:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded from Google's font servers, which may process your IP address as part of that request. We do not control this processing; Google's own privacy policy applies.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted by GitHub, Inc., which has its own privacy practices for hosting and delivering the site.
  • Formspree — our contact form submissions are processed and delivered to us by Formspree, Inc. See Formspree's privacy policy for how they handle that data.
  • Embedded client projects — our Portfolio page displays live previews of client/demo projects (e.g. Wear Drip, Luxora, Aldermere) inside embedded frames. Visiting or interacting with those previews may be subject to that project's own privacy policy and cookie practices, which we don't control.

Data Retention & Security

We retain any information you send us (such as emails) only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you or fulfil a project, and we take reasonable measures to keep it secure. As a small studio, we don't maintain a database of visitor information at this time.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information you've shared with us. To exercise any of these, contact hello@voryzaq.com and we'll respond as promptly as we can.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site or our practices change — for example, once the contact form is connected to a live backend, or if we add analytics. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@voryzaq.com.

Note: this is a general-purpose privacy policy template, not legal advice. Depending on where your business is registered and where your clients/visitors are located, you may be subject to specific laws (for example, the EU/UK GDPR, California's CCPA, or Pakistan's Personal Data Protection framework once enacted). We'd recommend a quick review by a local lawyer before relying on this as your final policy, especially once the contact form is connected to a real backend or if you add analytics.
